# My Chinsali

I have been thinking about an idea that could help strengthen community participation in local governance and make it easier for people to communicate their needs and concerns.

For instance, residents could use a digital platform to suggest projects their communities need, such as boreholes, better roads, improved sanitation, or other essential services. They could also quickly report incidents or challenges within their communities using their mobile phones.

For those with smartphones, this could be made possible through a mobile application and a dedicated Chinsali website. The website could serve as a central information hub where ongoing projects, completed works, government initiatives, and development progress are regularly published. This would promote transparency and accountability while giving residents an opportunity to provide feedback, ask questions, and commend the work being done.

For those using feature phones, the same services could be made accessible through USSD codes and SMS. For example, residents could dial *1234# to report an issue, suggest a community project, or participate in a poll. The local government could also use these channels to conduct community surveys, allowing residents to vote on which projects or challenges they believe should be prioritised.
